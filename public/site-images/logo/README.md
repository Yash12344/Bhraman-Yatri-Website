# Your logo goes here

**You do not need to know any coding to do this.**

Put your logo file in this folder and name it **`logo`**. That's it — the
website picks it up on its own and shows it in the top bar and in the footer.

    logo.png

Accepted types: `.svg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.jpg`, `.avif`

Until you add a file, the website shows a simple built-in mountain logo with
"BHRAMAN YATRI" written beside it. As soon as `logo.png` exists here, that is
replaced by your file.

---

## Important: your file should include your name

Your logo replaces **the whole thing** — the picture *and* the words "BHRAMAN
YATRI — EXPLORE BEYOND LIMITS" next to it. So use the version of your logo that
already has your business name in it, not just the symbol on its own.

## What shape and size?

Any shape works — wide, square or tall. The website keeps your logo's
proportions and fits it to the right height automatically.

- **Best format: `.png` with a transparent background**, or `.svg` if your
  designer gave you one. A transparent background means no white box around
  your logo.
- **Make it reasonably large** — around 600 pixels wide is plenty. Small
  images look blurry on modern phone and laptop screens.

## If your logo is dark

The footer at the bottom of every page has a **dark green background**, so a
dark logo can disappear against it. If that happens, add a second, lighter
version of your logo (usually the all-white one your designer provided) and
name it:

    logo-light.png

The website will use `logo-light` in the footer and `logo` everywhere else. If
you don't add one, your normal logo is used in both places.

---

## The little icon in the browser tab

That icon (the "favicon") is a separate file and lives outside this folder, at
`app/icon.svg`. Replacing it needs your developer — it's a one-minute job for
them.

## After adding your logo

The website needs to be published again (your developer or hosting provider
calls this "deploy") for it to appear online. If you still see the old logo
afterwards, do a hard refresh: **Ctrl + F5** on Windows, **Cmd + Shift + R** on
a Mac.
